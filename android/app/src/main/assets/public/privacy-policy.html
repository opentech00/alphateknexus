Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

All Apps

This Privacy Policy applies to all AlphaTek Nexus applications: the Client Portal, Admin Dashboard, Employee Portal, and Field Staff App. By using any of our services, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Client Portal

  • Account data: Full name, email address, phone number, and password (securely hashed).
  • Booking data: Service preferences, scheduling details, delivery addresses, and booking notes.
  • Payment data: Transaction references and payment method metadata (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Communication: Messages exchanged with our team through the portal.
  • Documents: Files you upload (e.g., customs documents for clearing & forwarding).

1.2 Admin Dashboard

  • Admin credentials: Email and password for dashboard access.
  • Activity logs: Login times, IP addresses, session data, and administrative actions.
  • Two-factor authentication: TOTP secret and recovery codes (encrypted at rest).

1.3 Employee Portal & Field Staff App

  • Employee records: Name, ID number, contact details, position, division, role, and employment status.
  • Identity documents: Government-issued ID copies and profile photos (stored in encrypted storage).
  • Location data: GPS coordinates collected only while you are clocked in and actively assigned to a field job.
  • Activity data: Attendance records, job check-ins, completion times, and incident reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the services you request (bookings, subscriptions, dispatch assignments).
  • To process payments and issue receipts and invoices.
  • To communicate with you about your bookings, payments, and account.
  • To send push notifications, emails, and in-app alerts based on your notification preferences.
  • To verify employee identity and manage access permissions.
  • To track field staff location during active assignments for dispatch coordination and safety.
  • To generate analytics and improve our service quality.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and auditing requirements.

3. Push Notifications & Communication

We send notifications across three channels: in-app alerts, email, and mobile push notifications. You can customize which types of notifications you receive and through which channels in your account settings. Push notifications require your explicit permission and can be revoked at any time through your device settings or in-app preferences.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers: Payment processors (Monime), email delivery (Resend), and push delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — each under strict data protection agreements.
  • Internal teams: Authorized administrators and your assigned field staff, strictly on a need-to-know basis.
  • Legal authorities: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our staff and clients.

5. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plaintext.
  • Row-level security policies enforce that users can only access their own data.
  • Admin sessions are protected by two-factor authentication and session management controls.
  • Identity documents are stored in access-controlled, encrypted storage buckets.

6. Data Retention

  • Client accounts: Retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Booking & payment records: Retained for 7 years for tax and audit compliance.
  • Employee records: Retained for the duration of employment plus 5 years per labor regulations.
  • Location data: Deleted within 90 days after the field assignment is completed.
  • Activity logs: Retained for 1 year for security auditing.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Notification preferences: Control which notifications you receive and through which channels.
  • Data portability: Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@alphateknexus.com.

8. Location Tracking (Field Staff App)

Location data is collected only while a field staff member is clocked in and assigned to an active job. The app uses geofencing to record arrivals and departures at job sites. Location history is visible to administrators for dispatch coordination and safety monitoring. All location data is automatically purged 90 days after the assignment ends.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification at least 30 days before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact:

  • Email: privacy@alphateknexus.com
  • AlphaTek Nexus — Data Protection Officer
